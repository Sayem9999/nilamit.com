# Nilamit Trust & Safety Policy (v1.0)

## 1. Introduction
Nilamit is a community-driven auction marketplace. Our mission is to provide a safe, transparent, and fair environment for buyers and sellers in Bangladesh. This policy governs the use of our Escrow and Logistics protection systems.

## 2. Escrow & Payment
- All winning bids must be paid into the Nilamit Escrow system.
- Funds are held securely until the Buyer confirms receipt of the item or the 72-hour auto-release window expires.
- **Nilamit does not handle logistics directly** but provides the financial infrastructure to secure the transaction.

## 3. Seller Protection (Zero-Loss Logistics)
To protect sellers from frivolous rejections (RTO - Return to Origin), the following rules apply:
- **Invalid Rejections**: If a Buyer rejects an item at the doorstep for reasons other than "Item Not as Described" (e.g., changed mind, found elsewhere, no cash), a **120 BDT Logistics Fee** will be deducted from the Buyer's refund.
- **Forfeiture**: This 120 BDT is transferred directly to the Seller to compensate for courier and packaging losses.
- **Consent**: By paying into Escrow, the Buyer explicitly consents to this potential deduction.

## 4. Buyer Protection (Authenticity Guarantee)
- **Valid Disputes**: If an item arrives broken, significantly different from the listing, or is a counterfeit not disclosed as such, the Buyer is entitled to a **100% Full Refund**.
- **Evidence**: Buyers must provide clear photo/video evidence during the dispute process within the 24-hour inspection window.

## 5. Prohibited Behavior
- **Shill Bidding**: Artificially inflating prices using alternate accounts or friends is strictly forbidden.
- **Off-Platform Transactions**: Attempting to bypass the Escrow system by dealing directly with a user found on Nilamit is a violation of our terms and will result in a permanent ban.
- **Misrepresentation**: Deliberately hiding defects or using stock photos for used items is prohibited.

## 6. Reputation & Verification
- Users with high rejection rates will have their accounts restricted.
- Verified Seller status requires a secondary verification process (NID/Social verification) and provides higher trust badges.
