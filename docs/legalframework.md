# ⚖️ Nilamit Legal Framework (v1.0)

> Last Updated: April 29, 2026

This document establishes the legal boundaries and regulatory compliance standards for Nilamit, a C2C auction marketplace operating in Bangladesh.

---

## 1. Regulatory Governance
- **Digital Commerce Operational Guidelines, 2021**: Primary directive for all marketplace operations.
- **The Consumers' Rights Protection Act, 2009**: Governing product quality and delivery accountability.
- **Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Act, 2006**: Establishing the legality of digital contracts and electronic records.
- **Cyber Security Act, 2023**: Governing data protection and cybercrime prevention.

---

## 2. Auction & Bidding Rules
- **Shill Bidding**: Strictly prohibited. Any attempt to artificially inflate prices via secondary accounts will result in a permanent ban.
- **Bid Binding**: A winning bid constitutes a legally binding contract under the ICT Act 2006.
- **Soft-Close (Anti-Snipe)**: Clearly defined as a mechanism to ensure fairness and prevent network-latency exploitation.
- **Seller Accuracy**: Sellers are legally liable for the accuracy of item descriptions, conditions, and geographic location tags.

---

## 3. Escrow & Financial Compliance
- **Advanced Payment (Escrow Mandate)**: Per Bangladesh Bank regulations, any advanced payment (including Bid Deposits) exceeding 10% of the transaction value must be held in a regulated escrow state.
- **Escrow Stages**:
    *   **HELD**: Funds are captured but not accessible to the seller.
    *   **RELEASED**: Funds sent to seller after buyer confirms "Item Received" or 10 days pass without dispute.
    *   **REFUNDED**: Funds returned to buyer if the seller fails to ship within 48 hours.

---

## 4. Privacy & Data Integrity
- **Prior Consent**: All personal data collection (Phone, Identity) is performed only after explicit, affirmative consent.
- **Data Retention**: Under Directive 2021, Nilamit maintains immutable logs of all transactions and communications for **6 years**.
- **Data Security**: Hashing of sensitive IDs and encrypted storage of payment identifiers.

---

## 5. Dispute Resolution (The 72-Hour Rule)
- **Complaint Management**: Nilamit provides a centralized "Report Auction" and "Dispute Transaction" mechanism.
- **Resolution Timeline**: 
    1. **Acknowledge**: All complaints must be acknowledged within **24 hours**.
    2. **Resolve**: Initial resolution or administrative mediation must be initiated within **72 hours** as mandated by the Digital Commerce Guidelines.

---

## 6. Prohibited Content & Listings
The following items are strictly forbidden from the platform:
- **Illegal Goods**: Any item prohibited by the laws of Bangladesh.
- **Addictive Substances**: Tobacco, alcohol, or illicit drugs.
- **Medicines**: Prescription medicines or unlicensed health products.
- **Gambling**: Services or items facilitating online betting.
- **MLM**: Multi-level marketing products or service opportunities.

---

## 7. Direct Pickup & Area Filtering
Nilamit facilitates hyper-local (Areas) and community (Circles) trading. While the platform provides the digital venue, users are responsible for their safety during physical handovers. We recommend meeting in well-lit, public locations for "Local Pickup" transactions.

---

## 8. Intellectual Property & Counterfeits
- **Zero Tolerance**: The sale of counterfeit, pirated, or unauthorized replica goods is strictly prohibited.
- **Takedown Policy**: Nilamit will remove any listing upon receipt of a valid IPR infringement notice from a brand owner or authorized representative.
- **Reporting**: Users are encouraged to report suspicious items using the "IPR Infringement" category in the report tool.

---

## 9. Platform Role & Liability (Section 79)
- **Intermediary Status**: Nilamit acts solely as an intermediary facilitator (marketplace). We do not take ownership of items, nor do we guarantee the quality or legality of user-generated listings.
- **ICT Act Compliance**: In accordance with Section 79 of the ICT Act 2006, Nilamit is not liable for third-party content provided it remains within the boundaries of a passive host.
- **User Accountability**: Every user is legally liable for their own listings, bids, and communications.

---

## 10. Eligibility & Contracts
- **Minimum Age**: Users must be at least **18 years of age** to register, bid, or list items. This ensures all bids are enforceable as binding contracts.
- **VAT on Commission**: All platform service fees are subject to 5% or 15% VAT (as applicable by NBR), which will be automatically deducted from the escrow settlement.
