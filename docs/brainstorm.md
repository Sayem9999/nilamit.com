# 🧠 Nilamit: Future Brainstorming (Phase 4 & Beyond)

> Last Updated: April 29, 2026

This document serves as a "Living Laboratory" for high-potential features, architectural pivots, and moonshot ideas for the Nilamit marketplace.

---

## 🤖 1. AI & Accessibility (Bengali-First)
- **Auto-Moderator**: Proactive detection of "Scam" or "Counterfeit" listings using multimodal (Image + Text) analysis.
- **Smart Valuation**: Train a model on auction history to suggest a "Starting Price" and "Reserve Price" for sellers based on current market heat.

## 🛡️ 2. Advanced Trust & Security
- **Identity Hardening (Tier 2)**: NID,phone,email verification for "nidVerified" ""phoneVerified" "email verified" seller badges .
- **Smart Escrow 2.0**: Integration with Mobile Financial Services (MFS) like bkash and nagad 

## 🚛 3. The Coordination & Logistics Layer
- **Courier Connect**: Direct integration API with RedX/Pathao/Steadfast. One-click booking from the seller dashboard once an auction is won.
- **PSSA 2.0 Compliance**: Automated digital receipts and tax-ready transaction logs for professional merchants.

## ⚡ 4. Marketplace Efficiency
- **Bulk Import for Shops**: CSV/API support for professional surplus sellers to list 100+ items instantly.
- **Bidder "Fast-Pass"**: Allow recurring high-reputation bidders to participate in high-value auctions without per-auction phone OTP verification.

## 🗺️ 5. Social Convergence (Re-Imagining Circles)
- **Verified Brands**: Official storefronts for verified Bangladeshi manufacturers/importers.
- **Community Interest Feeds**: "Vintage Dhaka" or "Student Tech Garage" — curated global feeds based on interest rather than hyper-local neighborhoods.

---
> [!TIP]
> Prioritize features that **reduce the "Trust Gap"** without adding friction to the "English-First" web and mobile experience.
